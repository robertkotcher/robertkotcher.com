NYC school districts by commute

Public school districts with an estimated weekday transit trip of 45 minutes or less to Manhattan.

Method and sources

Estimates use the fastest modeled train, subway, bus or walking trip from any part of a district to Penn Station, Grand Central, Port Authority or World Trade Center. The screen uses scheduled weekday arrivals from 7:30 to 9:35 a.m. It includes transfer waits and estimated walks, with up to 12 minutes of walking to the first stop. It excludes driving, ferries and delays.

District outlines show the full district. Coverage estimates the share of its mapped area within reach and can include nonresidential land or water. Check your exact address and departure time before relying on a result.

Schedules use November 17, 2026, except PATH, which uses a November 2025 weekday schedule as a proxy. A district outside the cutoff has no qualifying trip in this model.

Schedules: MTA, NJ Transit, PATH feed. Boundaries: 2025 Census, NYC Open Data. Ranks: 2027 Niche statewide rankings for NJ and NY.
